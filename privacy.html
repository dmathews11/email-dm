Email DM privacy policy

Email DM is a personal Gmail client used by its developer for their own Google account. It has no other users.

The app accesses Gmail through Google's Gmail API with the owner's consent, using the gmail.modify and gmail.settings.basic scopes, to read, label, archive, trash, and manage filters for the owner's mail.

Mail and account data are stored only on the owner's own devices. No data is sent to any server operated by the developer or to any third party. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which deletes the stored authorisation.

Email DM's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Last updated 18 October 2026.
